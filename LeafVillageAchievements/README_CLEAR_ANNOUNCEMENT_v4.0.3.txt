LeafVillageAchievements v4.0.3

- Dungeon and raid clear achievements now require only the normal clear roster.
- Rare, random, summoned, rotating, and other optional encounters no longer block a clear.
- Optional bosses are still tracked and can still award their individual achievements.
- Individual raid-boss achievements and any boss-count milestones triggered by a raid kill show locally and do not announce in guild chat.
- The raid full-clear achievement is the only achievement from that kill that announces in guild chat.
- Existing saved boss progress is rechecked silently on login against the corrected clear criteria.
