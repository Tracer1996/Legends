Ashen Banner Collection Achievements 4.0.1

- Mount and companion collections are integrated into LeafVillageAchievements.
- Every recognized mount and companion has an individual achievement.
- Individual rewards use 25-point tiers: 25 / 50 / 75 / 100.
- Existing companion achievements were upgraded from 1 point.
- Mount and companion collection milestones are included.
- Learned mounts and companions award after the initial silent seed scan.
- Companion and mount searches are isolated to their own tabs.
- Companion scrolling no longer exposes achievement rows.
- The duplicate Mounts tab label has been removed.
