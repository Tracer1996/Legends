ASHEN ANNOUNCEMENT FILTER
Version 1.0.0

Purpose
- Locally hides The Ashen Banner achievement and badge announcements from guild chat.
- Does not stop announcements from being sent.
- Does not affect what other guild members see.
- Does not interfere with achievement or badge records.

Installation
1. Extract the AshenAnnouncementFilter folder into Interface/AddOns.
2. Restart the game or type /reload.
3. Click the minimap button to choose what to hide.

Minimap Button
- Left-click: Open settings.
- Right-click: Quickly hide or show both announcement types.
- Drag: Move the button around the minimap.

Commands
/ashenfilter
/aaf
/aaf hide
/aaf show
/aaf status

Saved Settings
- Achievement filter choice
- Badge filter choice
- Minimap button position

The settings are account-wide and automatically persist between logins.
